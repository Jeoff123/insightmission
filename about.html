Our Journey

Milestones that mark our journey of growth and impact

Foundation

1998

Insight Mission was founded in Mavelikara, Kerala, by Sunil D. Kuruvilla—an acclaimed trainer and educator—laying the foundation for a transformative Human Resource Development initiative.

Early Outreach

2000

Began conducting career guidance and life enrichment sessions in schools and colleges, reaching hundreds of students across Kerala.

Growth & Recognition

2008

Expanded reach across the state with increasing demand for Insight Mission’s unique and practical approach to youth empowerment.

Milestone Achieved

2014

Surpassed 2,000 training sessions, marking a significant milestone in building life skills, values, and career clarity among young people.

Adapting to Change

2020

Transitioned to digital platforms during the pandemic, offering online mentoring, webinars, and virtual workshops to stay connected with youth.

Present Day

2025

With over 26 years of service and more than 4,200 impactful sessions, Insight Mission continues to inspire and empower youth across Kerala and beyond.
